# Social comments — Student guide

On some pages of your courses, the Social comments block lets you talk with your teachers and the
other participants about that page: ask a question, answer one, share what you tried. On activity
pages, the block is in the block drawer on the right; open it with the drawer button if it is
closed.

## Commenting and replying

- Write into the text field at the top of the block and choose **Post**.
- Choose **Reply** below a comment to answer it.
- Use the menu (⋮) of your own comments and replies to **Edit** or **Delete** them. Deleting a
  comment deletes the replies to it too.

Everybody who can see the page can read your comments. In group work with separate groups, your
comments are seen by your group and your teachers; the block shows the group of each comment.

## Pinning

Pin a comment you want to find again, or use the pin at the bottom of the block to pin all comments
of the page. **Pinboard** shows everything you pinned in the course, for example to revise before
an exam. Nobody else sees your pins.

## Following a page

Turn on **Subscribe** to get a digest of the new comments and replies on the page as a notification.
Posting a comment subscribes you automatically; turn the switch off to stop the digest. Choose how
you get the digests (in Moodle, by email or in the app) in your notification preferences, under
*Digest of new comments on pages you subscribed to*.

## New comments

**Comments report** at the bottom of the block lists the comments posted since your last login,
page by page.
